Pumpwise: Baby Feeding Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-05

Pumpwise is developed by Daniel Esteban Gonzalez, Argentina ("we", "us"). In short: what you log about your baby and yourself stays on your device. We never receive it, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Information the app stores

The app stores what you enter: pumping sessions, feeds, your milk stash, diapers, sleep, growth measurements, vaccines, appointments with your questions and notes, health notes (such as a temperature or a medicine you gave, as you write it), first moments, the photos you choose to attach, your baby's name and optional date of birth, and your settings. This information is stored only on your device, in a storage area shared with the app's own widgets and Live Activity on the same device. We have no servers and never receive, see or store it.

Health information

Some of what you log is health information about your baby or about you. It is treated like everything else in the app: it stays on your device, is not used for advertising or data mining, and is never sent to us. The app does not read or write Apple Health data.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share information.

Apple Intelligence features

Some optional features use Apple Intelligence: turning a sentence into a log, summaries, handoff notes, tips, preparing a visit and questions about your log. When you use one, the text needed for that request (what you typed or dictated, and the entries and numbers from your log that the feature needs) is processed by Apple's model on your device or, when available, by Apple's Private Cloud Compute. According to Apple, data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to fulfill the request, is not stored and is not accessible to Apple. We never receive this data. You can turn these features off in Settings, and the app works fully without them. See https://www.apple.com/privacy/.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the app with Siri or Shortcuts, your request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app only answers with information that is already on your device.

Files and notes you share

When you export or share something (a PDF report, a CSV file or a handoff note), it is created on your device and sent only where you choose, using the system share sheet. If you add an appointment to your Calendar, it is created with the system's calendar sheet. What happens afterwards depends on the app or person you send it to.

Photos

If you attach a photo (for example of a vaccine card), you choose it with the system photo picker. The app only receives the photos you pick and stores a copy inside the app on your device.

Notifications

The app may ask to send notifications, only for the reminders you turn on (pumping, sanitizing, bags to use soon, appointments and doses you added). Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can change this anytime in the Settings app.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual entries in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its information from your device. Your device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's information until those backups are replaced.

Children

The app is made for parents and caregivers, not for children. Information you enter about your baby stays on your device; we do not collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or keep personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have questions, including under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 25,326) or the privacy laws of your country, contact us.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new effective date. Contact: Daniel Esteban Gonzalez, deg.leon.2024@gmail.com
