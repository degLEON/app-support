Questions

Good to know.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Logging with a sentence, summaries, handoff notes, today's tip, preparing the visit and asking your log use Apple Intelligence when it is available and turned on. Turn it off and every feature still works with Pumpwise's own calculation. Each result says who wrote it.

Which Siri phrases can I use?

For example “Start pumping in Pumpwise”, “Switch sides in Pumpwise”, “How much milk is in my stash in Pumpwise?”, “When was the last feeding in Pumpwise?” and “Summarize the last day in Pumpwise”. More than 30 actions also appear in the Shortcuts app and Spotlight.

Where do the storage times come from?

From the guide you choose: the CDC, NHS, ABM, Germany's National Breastfeeding Committee, KDCA, Spain's Ministry of Health or Argentina's Ministry of Health. Each keeps its own times and conditions, with its source. Use-by labels are reminders based on that guide, not a guarantee about the milk.

Is this medical advice?

No. Pumpwise is a record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose or give medical or lactation advice; storage times come from the sources cited. Talk to your pediatrician about your baby's health.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. There is no account and no server of ours. You can share your log as a PDF report or CSV files whenever you want.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and get every feature, with no in-app purchases.
