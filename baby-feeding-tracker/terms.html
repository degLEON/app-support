What the app is

Pumpwise is a log for pumping, feeds, stored breast milk, diapers, sleep and your baby's growth, vaccines, appointments and health notes, with guides based on public health authorities. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical, lactation or nutritional advice, and it does not replace your midwife, health visitor, pediatrician or other health professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Guides and storage times

Storage times and other guidance are shown as published by the authority you choose, with its source and date. Authorities differ and can update their advice. Use-by labels are reminders based on the times you entered and the guide you chose; they are not a guarantee about the milk. When in doubt, ask your care team.

AI-generated content

Optional features use Apple Intelligence to turn sentences into draft entries, write summaries and notes, pick tips from the app's guides and answer questions about your log. AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Check it before relying on it or sharing it. Nothing is saved until you confirm, and numbers are calculated by the app from your own records.
