Questions

Good to know.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Summaries, reading a shift from a sentence and the export check use Apple Intelligence when your iPhone has it. Without it, the app shows the built-in calculated version, so everything keeps working.

Which Siri phrases can I use?

“Clock in with Work Hour Tracker”, “Clock out with Work Hour Tracker”, “How many hours this week?”, “Summarize my week” and “Export my hours”. You'll also find them in the Shortcuts app and Spotlight.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. There is no account and no server. You can export your log as CSV, PDF or plain text whenever you want.

Does it calculate my real paycheck?

It estimates earnings and overtime from the rates and threshold you set. It is a personal record-keeping tool, not legal, payroll or tax advice.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and get every feature, with no in-app purchases.
