What the app is

A personal log of your work hours. Totals, overtime and earnings are estimates for your own reference, calculated from what you enter and the rules you choose. They are not payroll, tax or legal advice and do not replace your employer's records or a professional.

AI-generated content

Optional features use Apple Intelligence to write summaries, read a sentence into a shift, or explain notes before exporting. AI output can be incomplete or wrong: check it before saving, relying on it or sharing it. The numbers are calculated by the app from your records; the AI only puts them into words, and a shift read from a sentence is never saved until you tap Save.
