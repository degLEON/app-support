Questions

Good to know.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Describe the job, Photo to plan, Project builder and the explanations use Apple Intelligence when your device has it, on device or with Private Cloud Compute. You can turn it off in Settings, and everything works without it.

Can the AI get a measurement wrong?

The AI never does the math. It only fills in the tool from your words or photo, and you can review what it filled in. Every number comes from TapeMark's exact fraction engine.

Which Siri phrases can I use?

“Calculate with TapeMark”, “Space shelves with TapeMark”, “Hang pictures with TapeMark”, “Start Mark Mode in TapeMark”, “Next mark in TapeMark”, “Add a cut in TapeMark” and “How many boards in TapeMark”. You'll also find them in Shortcuts and Spotlight.

Does it handle metric?

Yes. Mix feet, inches, fractions, centimeters and millimeters in one line, and round to 1/32, 1/16, 1/8, 1/4 or 1 mm.

Where are my projects stored?

On your device. There is no account and we never receive your projects or measurements.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once (USD 9.99) and get every feature, with no in-app purchases and no ads.

Should I still measure twice?

Yes. Results are exact for the numbers you enter. Measure twice and check before you cut or drill.
