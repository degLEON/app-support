What the app is

TapeMark is a calculator and layout tool for measurements in feet, inches, fractions and metric units. It works out results, spacing, picture-hanging marks, cut lists, boards to buy and material estimates from the numbers you enter. Check before you cut or drill. Results are exact for the numbers you enter, then rounded to the precision you choose; they depend on your measurements being right. Material quantities, coverage and plan checks are estimates and general reminders for your own reference. They are not engineering, structural, electrical or building-code advice and do not replace a professional or the instructions of a product's manufacturer.

AI-generated content

Optional features use Apple Intelligence to set up tools from your description or photo, draft project parts, and write explanations and summaries. AI output can be incomplete or wrong. Review the inputs it fills in and every drafted part before relying on them. Measurements shown as results are always calculated by the app's own calculator, not by the AI.
