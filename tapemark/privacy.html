Privacy Policy

TapeMark: Tape Measure Calc · Effective 2026-11-19

TapeMark: Tape Measure Calc ("TapeMark", "the app") is developed by Daniel Esteban Gonzalez, Argentina ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: Your projects and measurements stay on your device. We never receive them, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Optional Apple Intelligence features may send the text or photo of a request to Apple to answer it.

Information the app stores

The app stores what you enter: your calculations and history, projects, cut lists and their cuts, spacing and picture layouts, area and material estimates, your Mark Mode progress, and your settings (such as units, rounding, eye-level height and saw kerf). This information is stored only on your device, in a storage area shared with the app's own widgets, controls and Live Activity on the same device. We have no servers and never receive, see or store it.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share information. The app does not use your location.

Apple Intelligence features

Some optional features use Apple Intelligence: Describe the job, Photo to plan, Project builder, Check my plan, Explain my answer and Shopping summary. When you use one, what that request needs (the words you type or dictate, the photo you choose, or the measurements of the layout or list you are working on) is processed by Apple's model on your device or, when available, by Apple's Private Cloud Compute. According to Apple, data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to fulfill the request, is not stored and is not accessible to Apple. We never receive this data. You can turn these features off in Settings, and the app works fully without them. See Apple's privacy information at https://www.apple.com/privacy/.

Photos and camera

Photo to plan is optional. If you use it, you choose a photo with the system photo picker (which gives the app only that photo) or take one with the camera after you allow camera access. The photo is read only to find the measurements written on it, on your device or by Apple Intelligence as described above. The app does not save the photo or send it to us. You can change camera access anytime in the iPhone Settings app.

Siri, Shortcuts and Visual Intelligence

If you use the app with Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight or Visual Intelligence, your request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app answers only with information that is already on your device or that it calculates from your request.

Files you share

When you share a result, a layout or a cut list, it is created on your device and sent only where you choose, using the system share sheet. What happens to it afterwards depends on the app or person you send it to.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual calculations, projects, lists and layouts in the app, and clear your history. Deleting the app removes all of its information from your device. Your device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's information until those backups are replaced.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or keep personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have questions, including under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 25,326) or the privacy laws of your country, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new effective date.
