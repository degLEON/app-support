What the app is

Step Sprout helps you break tasks into small steps, do them one at a time with a visual timer, sort what is on your mind, and rewrite or read the tone of messages. It is a productivity tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or replace care for ADHD, autism or any other condition. Time estimates are estimates for your own reference and can be wrong. Steps for tasks such as taxes, bills or documents are practical organizing steps only; they are not tax, legal, financial or medical advice and do not replace official instructions or a professional.

AI-generated content

Optional features use Apple Intelligence to suggest steps, sort tasks, rewrite text and describe how a message may sound. AI output can be incomplete or wrong, and the tone of a written message can be read in more than one way. Check suggestions before relying on them or sending them. You can edit every step, and you can use the app without AI.
