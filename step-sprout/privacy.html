Step Sprout: ADHD Task Helper — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-19

Step Sprout: ADHD Task Helper ("Step Sprout", "the app") is developed by Daniel Esteban Gonzalez, Argentina ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: your tasks stay on your devices and in your own private iCloud. We never receive them, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Information the app stores

The app stores what you enter and what it measures for you: your tasks and their steps, the minutes you estimated and the minutes steps really took, which steps you finished or skipped, the reasons you chose when you were stuck, the tasks created from a brain dump, your energy level choice, and your settings (such as timer style, vibration and whether AI features are on). This information is stored on your device, in a storage area shared with the app's own widgets, Live Activity and share extension on the same device. If iCloud is turned on for the app in your device settings, it is also synced through your own private iCloud database so your iPhone and iPad show the same tasks; Apple stores it under your Apple Account and we cannot access it. We have no servers and never receive, see or store this information. Photos you use to split a task and messages you paste into the message helper are used only for that request. The app does not save the photo or the message; it saves only the steps or the rewritten text if you choose to keep them.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share information.

Apple Intelligence features

Some optional features use Apple Intelligence: splitting a task into steps, splitting from a photo, sorting a brain dump, rewriting a step when you are stuck, choosing what fits in the minutes you have, estimating time, and the message helper. When you use one, what that request needs (the task or step text, the dictated text, the photo, the message, or the titles and minutes of your tasks) is processed by Apple's model on your device or, when available, by Apple's Private Cloud Compute. According to Apple, data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to fulfill the request, is not stored and is not accessible to Apple. We never receive this data. The app also works fully without these features (library, splitting by hand, and built-in suggestions), and you can turn them off in Settings. See Apple's privacy information at https://www.apple.com/privacy/.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the app with Siri or Shortcuts, your request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app only answers with information that is already on your device. Your task titles can appear in Spotlight search on your device.

Text you share into the app

When you share text from another app into Step Sprout (for example, a message to rewrite or judge), it is used only on your device and for the AI request you start. What you copy or send back afterwards goes only where you choose.

Permissions

The app may ask for access to the microphone and speech recognition (to dictate a task or a brain dump; speech is turned into text on your device), the camera (to photograph something to split; you can also pick a photo, which needs no permission) and notifications (to tell you gently when a step's time is almost up). Each one is optional and used only for that purpose. You can change this anytime in the iPhone Settings app.

Deleting your information

You can delete tasks and steps in the app. Deleting the app removes its information from your device. If you use iCloud sync, you can delete the app's iCloud data in the Settings app (your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage). Your device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's information until those backups are replaced.

Health information

Step Sprout is a productivity tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, and it does not ask for health information.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or keep personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have questions, including under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 25,326) or the privacy laws of your country, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new effective date.
