Questions

Good to know.

Who is Step Sprout for?

Adults with ADHD and anyone who freezes before starting a task. It is a planning tool, not a medical device: it does not diagnose or treat ADHD or any other condition.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?

No. Apple Intelligence runs on your device or with Private Cloud Compute when it's available. Without it, everything still works: 48 ready-made tasks, splitting by hand, a built-in answer bank for I'm stuck and tone templates for messages. You can turn the AI features off in Settings.

Which Siri phrases can I use?

“Split a task in Step Sprout”, “What's my next step in Step Sprout?”, “I'm done with this step in Step Sprout”, “I'm stuck in Step Sprout”, “Add five minutes in Step Sprout”, “Brain dump in Step Sprout”, “I have a few minutes in Step Sprout”, “How long will my task take in Step Sprout?”, “Start a ready-made task in Step Sprout” and “Just start in Step Sprout”. They're also in the Shortcuts app.

Are there streaks or scores?

No. Done today is a kind list of what you finished. Nothing withers and nothing resets.

Does it sync between my iPhone and iPad?

Yes, through your own private iCloud. We never receive your tasks.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay $6.99 USD once and get every feature, with no in-app purchases.
