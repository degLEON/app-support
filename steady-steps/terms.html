What the app is

The app offers gentle, Tai Chi-inspired walking and balance practices, a daily body check, guided walks, a practice log and simple progress views, based on the developer's own book. It is for general education and personal practice only.

Your safety and risk

You use the practices at your own risk and choose what is safe for you. Practice near stable support, on a clear and well-lit floor, and in supportive shoes. Stop immediately if you feel dizzy, faint, chest pain or pressure, sudden weakness, sudden vision changes, sharp pain, unusual shortness of breath or confusion, or if you lose control of your balance, and get help if needed. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Measurements and estimates

The steadiness check, pace feedback and the Apple Health trends shown in the app are estimates for your own reference. The steadiness check only compares you with your own earlier checks. None of these are a diagnosis, a measure of fall risk or a medical result, and the app makes no promise about outcomes such as preventing falls.

AI-generated content

Optional features use Apple Intelligence to suggest today's practice, answer questions from the app's content and summarize your week. AI output can be incomplete or wrong. It never overrides the safety rules: on Red days, or whenever you feel unwell, stop and rest. The app's numbers are calculated from your own records; the AI only puts them into words.
