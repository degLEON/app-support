Steady Steps: Tai Chi Walking — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-13

Steady Steps: Tai Chi Walking ("Steady Steps", "the app") is developed by Daniel Esteban Gonzalez, Argentina ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: everything you log stays on your device. We never receive it, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Information the app stores

The app stores what you enter and what it measures for you: your daily body checks (Green, Yellow or Red), the practices and walks you do, their length, the one-word logs, your confidence ratings, optional notes, your Confidence Ladder progress and badges, your steadiness check results, and your settings (such as chair mode, reminder time, voice and vibration). This information is stored only on your device, in a storage area shared with the app's own widgets and Live Activity on the same device. We have no servers and never receive, see or store it.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share information. The app does not use your location.

Apple Health

Apple Health is optional. Only if you allow it, the app saves your practice sessions to Apple Health (as mindful minutes and workouts) and reads your Walking Steadiness, walking speed and step length to show your own trends. Health data is read and used only on your device to show these trends and, if you choose, in a PDF summary you create. It never leaves your device through the app, is never sent to us, and is never used for advertising or data mining. The app never writes data to Apple Health that it did not record. You can change or remove access anytime in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Steady Steps, and you can delete saved samples in the Health app.

Motion & Fitness

Motion & Fitness is optional. Only if you allow it, the app uses your device's motion sensors and pedometer on your device to estimate your walking pace during guided walks (to suggest a slower pace) and to measure how still you stand during the steadiness check. These readings are processed on your device. Only the summary result of each steadiness check is saved, on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Apple Intelligence features

Some optional features use Apple Intelligence: Today's coach, Ask Steady and the weekly summary. When you use one, the text needed for that request (your body check, recent practice logs and notes, your question, or your weekly numbers, together with the app's own content) is processed by Apple's model on your device or, for some requests, by Apple's Private Cloud Compute. According to Apple, data sent to Private Cloud Compute is processed only to fulfill your request, is not stored and is not made accessible to Apple. We never receive this data. You can turn these features off in the app's Settings, and the app works fully without them. See Apple's privacy information at https://www.apple.com/privacy/.

Siri and Shortcuts

If you use the app with Siri, Shortcuts, the Action button or Control Center, your request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app only answers with or saves information on your device. Practice logs can be offered to the system as journal entries so that Siri and Shortcuts can find them on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No push notification service is used and nothing is sent to us.

Files you export or share

When you create a PDF summary, it is created on your device and contains only what you choose to include. It is sent only where you choose, using the system share sheet. What happens to it afterwards depends on the app or person you send it to.

Contact form

The Contact screen only opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. If you do, we receive what you write and your email address, and use it only to reply. You can ask us to delete your messages at deg.leon.2024@gmail.com.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual logs in the app, or everything in Settings > Delete all my data. Deleting the app removes all of its information from your device. Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. Your device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's information until those backups are replaced.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or keep personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side, except emails you choose to send us. If you have questions, including under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 25,326) or the privacy laws of your country, contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new effective date.
